About

Hi, I’m Chaianan, a junior web developer from Udon Thani, Thailand. I build web applications with Laravel and Django, with a focus on backend development, REST APIs, and database design. I’ve built an e-sports tournament system and a car rental system as university projects. I have also worked as a contract teacher, teaching graphic design and basic programming, and completed internships at a plastic cup screen-printing company and a computer repair shop (PN Computer), where I handled initial customer diagnosis and installed Windows and software.

My goal is to gain real-world experience and grow in a collaborative team. I want to write better code and use AI tools effectively, for debugging, learning new topics, and writing test cases I might miss. I’m also interested in building practical tools that solve everyday problems, such as an email management system that helps me track important messages, a personal finance tracker for expenses and taxes, and business management systems for the future.
